# EarlyWatch

## Early-Warning & Intervention Support Platform

A student usually doesn't decide to drop out overnight.

Low attendance, falling grades, missed assignments, increasing stress, financial difficulties, or other challenges can build up over time. The problem is that these warning signs are often scattered across different places, making it difficult for educators to notice when a student is starting to struggle.

**EarlyWatch brings these signals together and helps educators spot students who may need support earlier.**

The system uses student data to estimate dropout risk and, more importantly, explains **what is contributing to that risk**. It then connects those factors with possible ways an educator could support the student.

### How it works

**Student Signals → Risk → Contributing Factors → Possible Support → Follow-up**

For example, instead of simply showing:

> **High Risk — 88%**

EarlyWatch can help an educator see that the prediction is influenced by things such as low attendance, assignment delays, academic performance, or stress, along with possible support such as academic mentoring or an attendance follow-up.

### What EarlyWatch Provides

- **Teacher Dashboard** — A quick view of students who may need attention.
- **Risk Assessment** — A predicted risk level and probability for each student.
- **Explainable Factors** — Shows the factors contributing to the prediction.
- **Possible Support Actions** — Suggests relevant areas where an educator could intervene.
- **Student Profiles** — Brings a student's relevant information and risk insights together in one place.

### Built to Support, Not Label

EarlyWatch is not meant to decide a student's future.

A prediction is only a signal that a student **may need attention**. The educator remains responsible for understanding the student's situation and deciding what support is appropriate.

The platform does not label students or automatically intervene. It gives educators information they can use to make better, earlier decisions.

## Technology

- React + Vite
- Python
- FastAPI
- Machine Learning

## Our Goal

We want to move the conversation from:

**"Which students might drop out?"**

to:

**"Which students may be struggling, why might they be struggling, and how can we support them early?"**# EarlyWatch
